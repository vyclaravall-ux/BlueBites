Objectives & Success Metrics

Aligned with insights from user interviews, empathy maps, and journey maps.

Primary Goal

Create a platform that gives Atenean students a transparent, time‑efficient, and value‑oriented cafeteria experience.

Specific Objectives

  1. Enhance Menu Transparency
    • Provide verified listings for prices, portion sizes, and photos in collaboration with stall owners.
    • Enable reviews and feedback for verification via first‑hand experiences.
  2. Improve Decision‑Making Efficiency
    • Allow digital browsing of stalls with comparisons on serving sizes, prices, and options.
    • Mini‑map and real‑time updates on stall activity and waiting times.
    • Filters by budget and prep time; favorites for quick access.
  3. Support Budget‑Conscious Meal Planning
    • Filter by budget and display transparent prices.
    • Optional budget tracker to log spending and warn near limits.
  4. Encourage Healthy Eating Habits
    • Streamline find/compare/choose to reduce skipped meals.
    • Enable planning ahead via transparent info and filters.
  5. Foster Collaboration with Stall Owners
    • Gather insights from reviews to improve stalls.
    • Create a continuous feedback loop that lifts the overall campus dining experience.

Measurable Success Indicators

Menu Transparency
  • ≥ 60–70% of active stalls provide verified menus (prices, portions, photos) within 2 weeks.
  • Average stall rating ≥ 4.0/5 in first 2 weeks.
  • ≤ 0–3 complaints about misleading info in first 2 weeks.
Decision Efficiency
  • ≥ 70% decide on a meal in under 10 minutes (survey/interview) within 1 month.
  • ≥ 80% rate info “accurate/very accurate” on 4‑point scale within 2 weeks.
Budget Planning
  • ≥ 70% actively use budget filters and/or tracker within 1 month.
Healthy Habits
  • Streak system shows ≥ 70% with only 1–2 missed weekdays within first 2 weeks.
Collaboration
  • ≥ 50% of active stalls publish verified menus within 1 month.
  • ≥ 10 total user reviews/ratings across stalls within 1 month.

Key Features & Functional Scope

MVP — must‑have

  • Complete and accurate list of food stalls and general location (Gonzaga, JSEC, ISO).
  • Complete list of food options and prices per stall.
  • Filtering by price and preparation time.
User stories (MVP)
  • As a student, I can see available stalls to explore new options, increase satisfaction, and plan around classes.
  • As a student, I can see food options and prices per stall to match dietary needs and budget.
  • As a student, I can filter by price and prep time to choose quickly and save time.

Enhancements — nice‑to‑have

  • Interactive map with exact locations, directions, clickable markers, and real‑time waiting times.
  • Favorite button linking to liked items.
  • Real food photos and verified portion sizes.
  • User reviews with 5‑star rating and comments.
  • Budget tracker to set weekly budget and log spending.
User stories (Enhancements)
  • As a student, I navigate campus food options via a map to discover vendors and plan by waiting times.
  • As a student, I favorite items to personalize and quickly access preferred meals.
  • As a student, I rely on real photos and verified portions for transparent choices.
  • As a student, I read reviews to make informed decisions and find hidden gems.
  • As a student, I track spending to avoid overspending and reduce planning stress.

Technical & Design Requirements

Focus on essential parts; Google Analytics removed.

High‑level Architecture (Prototype)

Tools & Frameworks

  • Front‑end: React (preferred for UI) or Django templates. Styling via CSS; optional component libs.
  • Back‑end: Python/Django REST Framework for APIs.
  • Data: SQLite/PostgreSQL. Optional Google Sheets for initial data ingestion.
  • Maps: Google Maps (nice‑to‑have) for interactive markers and wait times.
  • Auth: Django auth or third‑party provider (future).
  • Hosting/Dev: Replit for prototyping and debugging.
