About Blue Bites

Discover, decide, and dine—Blue Bites makes it easy.

Blue Bite is a digital meal listing and comparison platform designed to give Atenean students a faster, smarter, and more transparent cafeteria experience. It lets students view menus, prices, portion sizes, and waiting times all in one place. This helps users plan their meals ahead of time, explore affordable options, and make dining choices that save time and money.

Elevator Pitch

Ateneans waste time and money deciding where to eat, dealing with inaccurate portions, and standing in long lines—Blue Bite solves this by providing a quick, efficient platform to view cafeteria information and plan meals.
